What Is BreathEaseX And How Does It Work?

Most people assume that if they are short of breath, their lungs are simply "weak." That is rarely the whole story — and it is why so many fixes never stick.

Over the years they settle on the alveoli — the tiny air sacs where oxygen crosses into your blood — and slowly build into a film, like grime caked on a window. Air still comes in. It just cannot get across. You breathe deeper and deeper and still feel like you are pulling air through a straw — which is exactly why so many people stop halfway up a flight of stairs even though nothing is "wrong" on paper.

That blocked crossing point is the oxygen handoff. Until it is cleared, no amount of breathing harder makes up for it.
