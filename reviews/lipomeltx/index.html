LipoMeltX Ingredients

Each LipoMeltX gummy contains a blend of natural, research-backed compounds, each selected for a specific role in supporting healthy metabolism, blood sugar balance, and appetite control:

  • Apple Cider Vinegar: Acetic acid from apple cider vinegar is associated with a healthier post-meal glucose response and a greater feeling of fullness, delivered here without the harsh taste or enamel concerns of liquid ACV.
  • Chromium: A mineral studied for its role in improving insulin sensitivity and stabilizing blood sugar levels after meals, helping prevent the glucose spikes that trigger fat storage and drive cravings.
  • Green Tea Leaf Extract: One of the most studied thermogenic compounds available, green tea naturally supports the body's core temperature and calorie burn, even at rest, and contains EGCG, an antioxidant associated with fat oxidation and steady energy.
  • Cinnamon Bark Extract: A traditional botanical with modern research behind it, cinnamon supports a balanced blood sugar response and helps blunt the cravings that tend to follow carb-heavy meals.
  • Pomegranate + Beet Root: Antioxidant and polyphenol-rich ingredients that support healthy circulation and natural energy, helping sustain momentum well past the first 30 days of use.

Together, these ingredients work to support the body's fat-burning signals, regulate blood sugar, reduce appetite, and support metabolic energy — all delivered in a single berry-flavored gummy that's easy to build into any daily routine.
